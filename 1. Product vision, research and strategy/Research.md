# 📋 Research.md — AI-Driven Product Research Document

> **👋 How to use this document (Read this first!)**
>
> This document helps you research your idea **before** you write a single line of code.
> Think of it like a treasure map — the more carefully you fill it out, the easier your journey from idea → first 500 customers will be.
>
> **Simple rules:**
> - Every section has a prompt (a question or instruction). Just answer it honestly.
> - Sections marked 🔴 **Required** must be filled before you move forward.
> - Sections marked 🟡 **Recommended** add depth but can be done later.
> - Sections marked 🟢 **Optional** are nice-to-have extras.
> - Whenever you see `[ Write your answer here ]` — that is your blank to fill.
> - Whenever you see a table — fill in every row. Add more rows if you need.
> - **No answer is wrong.** Honest "I don't know yet" is better than a made-up answer.
>
> **Who should fill this?**
> You — the founder, builder, or person with the idea. No team needed.
>
> **When is this document "done"?**
> When you can read it top to bottom and say: *"I know exactly what I'm building, for whom, and why it will work."*

### 5.2 Interview Questions Template

> ✏️ **Instructions:** Use these questions in your interviews. Copy them into a notes doc or Notion.

**Opening (2 min)**
1. Tell me about your day-to-day role — what does a typical Tuesday look like?
2. What tools do you rely on most?

**Problem Discovery (10 min)**
3. How do you currently handle `[ the problem you're solving ]`?
4. How much time does that take you each week?
5. What frustrates you most about the current way you do it?
6. Have you tried any tools or solutions? What happened?

**Validation (5 min)**
7. If this problem disappeared tomorrow, what would change for you?
8. Have you ever paid for a solution like this? How much?
9. If a tool existed that `[ your core value prop ]`, would you use it?

**Wrap-up (3 min)**
10. Is there anything else about this topic I should know?
11. Can I stay in touch as I build this?

### B. Glossary (For First-Time Founders)

> Quick definitions so you never get lost in jargon.

| Term | Plain-English Meaning |
|---|---|
| **TAM** | The total size of the market — everyone who could ever be your customer |
| **SaaS** | Software as a Service — a web app people pay monthly to use |
| **MVP** | Minimum Viable Product — the smallest version of your product that delivers real value |
| **Churn** | When a customer cancels or stops paying |
| **MRR** | Monthly Recurring Revenue — how much money you earn every month from subscriptions |
| **CAC** | Customer Acquisition Cost — how much it costs you to get one new customer |
| **NPS** | Net Promoter Score — a 0–100 score measuring how much users love your product |
| **Persona** | A fictional but realistic description of your ideal user |
| **Claude API** | The way your product communicates with Claude to get AI-powered responses |
| **Prompt** | The instructions you send to Claude to get the output you want |
| **Go/No-Go** | A checkpoint decision: should we move forward or stop and rethink? |
| **Unfair Advantage** | Something unique about you or your product that competitors can't easily copy |
| **Positioning** | How you explain what your product is and why it's different |
| **Activation** | When a new user experiences the core value of your product for the first time |
