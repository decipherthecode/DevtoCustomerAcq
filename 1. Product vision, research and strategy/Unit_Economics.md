# 📘 HOW TO FILL THIS DOCUMENT — READ THIS FIRST

> **🧒 New to unit economics? Start here.**  
> Imagine you open a lemonade stand. Unit economics answers four simple questions:  
> 1. **How much does one cup cost you to make?** (Cost)  
> 2. **How much do you charge for one cup?** (Price)  
> 3. **How long before a customer comes back — and how often?** (Retention)  
> 4. **Will you ever make more money than you spend?** (Profitability)  
>
> This document answers those same questions — for your AI-powered software product.

## 9.2 — Customer Lifetime Value (LTV)

📌 **What is LTV?**  
LTV = Total money one customer gives you before they stop using your product.

> 🧒 **Simple version:** If a customer pays $30/month and stays for 10 months → LTV = $30 × 10 = **$300**.

**Formula:** `LTV = ARPU × Gross Margin % × Average Customer Lifespan`

| Input | Value | How to find it |
|-------|-------|---------------|
| Average Revenue Per User (ARPU) | ✏️ `$___/mo` | Total MRR ÷ Paying Customers |
| Gross Margin % | ✏️ `___%` | See Section 9.4 |
| Monthly Churn Rate | ✏️ `___%` | Customers lost ÷ Total customers |
| Average Customer Lifespan (months) | ✏️ `___ months` | 1 ÷ Monthly Churn Rate |
| **LTV** | ✏️ `$___` | ARPU × GM% ÷ Churn Rate |

📋 **Example calculation:**
- ARPU = $49/mo
- Gross Margin = 72%
- Monthly Churn = 3%
- Lifespan = 1 ÷ 0.03 = 33 months
- **LTV = $49 × 72% ÷ 3% = $1,176**

## 9.4 — Gross Margin

📌 **What is Gross Margin?**  
The % of revenue left AFTER paying the direct costs to serve each customer (hosting, AI API calls, support, etc.).

> 🧒 **Simple version:** You sell lemonade for $1. Each cup costs $0.30 to make. Gross Margin = ($1 - $0.30) ÷ $1 = 70%.

**Formula:** `Gross Margin = (Revenue - COGS) ÷ Revenue × 100`

# SECTION 14 — Glossary

📌 **What is this?**  
Plain-English definitions for every business term in this document. If you're new to startups or finance, read this section first.

---

| Term | Plain-English Definition | Example |
|------|------------------------|---------|
| **ARPU** (Average Revenue Per User) | The average amount ONE customer pays you each month | 100 customers paying $2,900 total → ARPU = $29/mo |
| **ARR** (Annual Recurring Revenue) | Your total subscription revenue for a full year | MRR of $10,000 → ARR = $120,000 |
| **Burn Rate** | How much money you spend every month | Spending $30,000/mo on salaries + tools = $30K burn |
| **CAC** (Customer Acquisition Cost) | How much you spend to get ONE paying customer | Spend $1,000 on ads, get 10 customers → CAC = $100 |
| **Churn Rate** | % of customers who cancel each month | 100 customers, 3 cancel → churn = 3% |
| **COGS** (Cost of Goods Sold) | Direct costs to deliver your product | Hosting + LLM API + support = COGS |
| **Freemium** | Free product with paid upgrades | Spotify Free → Spotify Premium |
| **Gross Margin** | % of revenue left after COGS | Revenue $100, COGS $30 → 70% gross margin |
| **ICP** (Ideal Customer Profile) | The perfect customer who gets max value from your product | "28-year-old mid-career software engineer in the US" |
| **LLM** (Large Language Model) | The AI brain behind your product | Claude, GPT-4, Gemini |
| **LTV** (Lifetime Value) | Total money one customer pays before they leave | $29/mo × 20 months = $580 LTV |
| **LTV:CAC Ratio** | Comparison of earnings vs. cost to acquire | LTV $580, CAC $100 → ratio 5.8:1 |
| **MRR** (Monthly Recurring Revenue) | Total subscription revenue received each month | 100 customers × $29 = $2,900 MRR |
| **NPS** (Net Promoter Score) | Measures how likely customers are to recommend you | 0–100; above 50 is excellent |
| **NRR** (Net Revenue Retention) | Revenue growth from existing customers | 100% NRR = no growth from expansions; >100% = growing |
| **Payback Period** | How long it takes to recover the cost of acquiring a customer | CAC $100, earn $20/mo profit → payback 5 months |
| **Prompt** | The instruction sent to an AI model | "Write a professional summary for this resume:" |
| **Runway** | How many months you can operate before running out of money | $90,000 cash ÷ $10,000/mo net burn = 9 months runway |
| **SAM** | The portion of the market you can actually serve | TAM = all car buyers; SAM = electric car buyers in California |
| **SOM** | The portion of SAM you can win in 1–2 years | 2% of California electric car buyers = SOM |
| **TAM** | Total possible market if you had 100% share | "The global resume software market is $1.2B" |
| **Tokens** | The "words" (or word-parts) that AI models process and charge for | GPT charges per 1,000 tokens; "Hello world" ≈ 3 tokens |
| **Unit Economics** | The revenue and cost for ONE unit (customer, transaction, etc.) | For $29/mo customer: cost $9 to serve → $20 margin per unit |
| **Willingness to Pay** | Highest price a customer would accept before walking away | "Customers will pay up to $49/mo — above that they churn" |
