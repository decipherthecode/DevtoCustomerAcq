# 📖 How to Fill This Document

> Read this section first — it takes less than 3 minutes and will save you hours later.

## 🟢 The Golden Rules

1. **One sentence is better than one paragraph.** Be specific. Vague answers make every decision harder later.
2. **Fill it in order.** Each section builds on the one before it.
3. **Use plain language.** Write as if you are explaining it to a smart friend who has never heard of your product.
4. **Replace every `[PLACEHOLDER]` with your real answer.** If you genuinely don't know yet, write `TBD — revisit by [date]` so you don't forget.
5. **Every field has a 💡 Tip.** Read the tip before you write your answer — it shows you exactly what a good answer looks like.
6. **"N/A" is only allowed if a field truly cannot apply to your product.** If you are tempted to write N/A, ask yourself: "Am I skipping this because it doesn't apply, or because I haven't thought about it yet?"
7. **Update this document as the product evolves.** It is a living document, not a one-time exercise.

## 🔑 Quick-Start Checklist

Before you begin, gather these things:

- [ ] A one-line description of what your product does
- [ ] The name of the person who will own this document
- [ ] A rough idea of who your first customer is
- [ ] A target date for your first working version (even a rough one)
- [ ] Any AI tools or models you are planning to use (even if just ChatGPT/Claude)

## 16.1 Glossary

|-----------------------|-------------------------------------------------------------------------|
| **MVP**               | Minimum Viable Product — the simplest version of your product that delivers real value. |
| **ICP**               | Ideal Customer Profile — a detailed description of your perfect first customer. |
| **AI Agent**          | An AI system that can perform multi-step tasks autonomously, often using tools and APIs. |
| **RAG**               | Retrieval-Augmented Generation — an AI technique that combines search with text generation. |
| **Hallucination**     | When an AI model generates information that sounds convincing but is factually wrong. |
| **MRR**               | Monthly Recurring Revenue — the predictable revenue your business earns each month. |
| **CAC**               | Customer Acquisition Cost — the total cost to acquire one new paying customer. |
| **LTV**               | Lifetime Value — the total revenue a single customer generates over their entire time with you. |
| **NPS**               | Net Promoter Score — a measure of how likely users are to recommend your product (scale: -100 to +100). |
| **p95 Latency**       | The response time that 95% of requests complete within — a standard performance benchmark. |
| **GTM**               | Go-to-Market — the plan for how you launch your product and acquire customers. |
| **Churn**             | The rate at which customers cancel or stop using your product. |
| **JTBD**              | Jobs-to-be-Done — a framework for understanding what customers are really trying to achieve. |
| **Prompt Injection**  | A security attack where a malicious user inserts text that tricks an AI into doing something unintended. |
