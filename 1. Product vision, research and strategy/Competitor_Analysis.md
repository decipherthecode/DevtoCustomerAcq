## 4. 🎯 Scoring Guide (Read Before Filling Competitor Cards)

> 💡 *Every score uses a 1–5 scale. Use the same definition every time so your scores
> are honest and comparable. Do NOT score what you WISH were true — score what IS true today.*

| Score | What it means (say it to yourself like this) |
|---|---|
| ⭐ 1 | "This is genuinely terrible. A user would immediately get frustrated." |
| ⭐⭐ 2 | "It works sometimes, but there are obvious rough edges." |
| ⭐⭐⭐ 3 | "It's okay. Gets the job done. Nothing special." |
| ⭐⭐⭐⭐ 4 | "Really good. Most users would be satisfied." |
| ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐ 5 | "Best-in-class. Hard to imagine it being better right now." |

### Scoring Dimensions & Weights

> 💡 *Weights must add up to 100. Adjust them to match what YOUR customer cares most about.
> The suggested weights below are a starting point.*

| # | Dimension | Weight | Why It Matters |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | AI Model / Engine Quality | 15% | Powers everything; bad AI = bad product |
| 2 | Automation & Agent Capabilities | 15% | Agentic workflows are the new competitive moat |
| 3 | AI Accuracy / Hallucination Rate | 10% | Trust breaker — one wrong answer can lose a customer |
| 4 | Data Privacy & AI Compliance | 10% | GDPR, HIPAA, SOC 2 — deal-breaker for enterprises |
| 5 | Pricing & Packaging Value | 15% | The #1 reason users switch products |
| 6 | UX / Ease of Use | 10% | Especially critical for solo founders with no IT team |
| 7 | API & Integration Depth | 10% | Key for dev-platform and B2D products |
| 8 | Customer Support & Docs | 5% | Often overlooked; huge for retention |
| 9 | Market Traction & Reviews | 10% | Validates product-market fit |
| **TOTAL** | | **100%** | |

**How to calculate Weighted Score:**
```
Weighted Score = (Score_1 × 0.15) + (Score_2 × 0.15) + (Score_3 × 0.10) + ... + (Score_9 × 0.10)
Maximum possible = 5.0
```

## 7. 🗺️ White-Space Opportunity Map

> 💡 *This is the most important section in the entire document.
> A white space is something customers NEED that NO competitor does well yet.
> These are your best chances to win. Be brutally honest — only list real gaps.*

### How to spot white spaces
1. Look at the "Top complaints" in every competitor card — recurring complaints = white space.
2. Look for any dimension where ALL competitors scored 3 or below.
3. Ask: "What did customers say they WISH the product did?" on G2, Reddit, App Store reviews.
4. Ask: "What niche or persona is completely ignored by existing players?"
