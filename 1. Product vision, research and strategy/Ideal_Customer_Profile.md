# 📖 HOW TO FILL THIS DOCUMENT
> **Read this first — it will save you a lot of time!**

---

## 🌟 What Is This Document?

This is your **Ideal Customer Profile (ICP)** — a written description of the *perfect* customer for your product. Think of it like writing a detailed profile of your dream customer before you even build or sell anything.

It answers three big questions:
1. **Who** is buying your product?
2. **Why** are they buying it?
3. **How** do you find more of them?

---

## 🧒 How to Fill This (Even If You're a Beginner)

**Step-by-step instructions:**

1. **Read the section title** — each section starts with a 🔷 symbol and a clear name.
2. **Look at the example** — every blank has an example in `[brackets like this]`. The example shows you *what kind of answer* to write.
3. **Replace the example with your own answer** — delete the example text inside `[ ]` and write your real answer.
4. **If you don't know the answer yet** — write `TBD` (To Be Determined) and come back later. That's totally okay!
5. **Keep it honest** — there are no wrong answers. The more honest you are, the more useful this document becomes.
6. **Update it regularly** — this document is not one-and-done. You should update it as you learn more about your customers.

---

---

## 🗺️ What Phases Does This Cover?

This template follows the **AI-Driven Development Life Cycle (AI-DDLC)**:

```
Phase 1 → DISCOVER     : Define who your customer is before building anything
Phase 2 → BUILD        : Use your ICP to guide features and AI model choices
Phase 3 → TEST         : Validate with real users that match your ICP
Phase 4 → LAUNCH       : Use your ICP to target marketing and early sales
Phase 5 → GROW         : Track how well your first 500 customers fit your ICP
Phase 6 → REFINE       : Update your ICP based on what you learned
```

---

## ⏱️ How Long Should This Take?

- **First draft:** 1–2 hours (answer what you know, write TBD for the rest)
- **Full version:** After 5–10 customer interviews — 2–4 hours
- **Ongoing updates:** 30 minutes every month until you hit 500 customers

## 🔷 SECTION 8 — ICP QUALIFICATION CHECKLIST
### *(Green Flags ✅ & Red Flags 🚩)*

> ❓ **Why This Matters:** When you get a new lead or a potential customer contacts you, use this checklist to quickly decide: "Is this person our ideal customer or not?" This saves you from wasting time on people who will never buy or will churn quickly.

> 📌 **How to use:** Go through this checklist for any real prospect. Count the green flags and red flags. If they have 7+ green flags, they're likely a strong ICP fit.

---

### ✅ GREEN FLAGS — Strong ICP Fit Signals

Check each one that applies to the prospect:

**Company / Customer Fit**  
`☐` Company size matches our ICP range  
`☐` Industry is in our primary target vertical  
`☐` Geography matches our target region  
`☐` Budget matches our pricing range  
`☐` They have experienced the exact problem we solve in the last 90 days  

**Urgency & Motivation**  
`☐` There's a clear trigger event (funding, growth, regulation, team change)  
`☐` They have a deadline or business pressure to solve this NOW  
`☐` Someone internally is championing our product (an internal advocate)  
`☐` They've already tried alternatives and those alternatives failed  

**AI Readiness**  
`☐` They are at AI Stage 2 or above (Exploring or higher)  
`☐` They already use at least one AI tool in their workflow  
`☐` They have reasonably clean, centralized data  
`☐` No severe compliance blockers (or blockers we already support)  

**Buying Signals**  
`☐` Economic buyer is involved in the conversation  
`☐` They asked about pricing or started a trial  
`☐` They referred someone else to us  
`☐` Engagement is high (replies quickly, attends demos, asks detailed questions)  

**Score: __ / 18 green flags**

---

### 🚩 RED FLAGS — Poor ICP Fit Signals

Check each one that applies to the prospect:

**Wrong Fit**  
`☐` Company size is far outside our ICP range  
`☐` Industry we don't serve or have no case studies for  
`☐` Budget is 5x below our minimum viable price  
`☐` They want a feature that conflicts with our product direction  

**Low Urgency**  
`☐` "Just exploring — no timeline or budget approved"  
`☐` No internal champion; no one pushing for this internally  
`☐` Happy with the status quo; no clear pain  
`☐` They want extensive custom development before paying anything  

**AI Blockers**  
`☐` AI Stage 0 — not ready to use AI in any capacity  
`☐` Severe data compliance blocker we cannot currently support  
`☐` On-premise only requirement; we're cloud-only  
`☐` Leadership is actively anti-AI or has banned AI tools  

**Deal Risk**  
`☐` Long enterprise procurement cycle (12+ months) with no urgency  
`☐` Competitor is deeply embedded and has a multi-year contract  
`☐` Previously churned from our platform  
`☐` Decision-maker is unreachable; only speaking with an influencer  

**Score: __ / 16 red flags**

| **0–3 green flags, 10+ red flags** | 🔴 **Outside ICP** — Politely decline or redirect. Not worth your time or theirs. |

## 📎 APPENDIX — QUICK REFERENCE GLOSSARY

> 📌 **For Beginners:** If you see a word in this document you don't understand, look it up here first.

| Term | Plain English Definition |
|------|-------------------------|
| **ICP** | Ideal Customer Profile — a detailed description of your perfect customer |
| **Firmographics** | Facts about a company (size, industry, revenue) — like demographics but for businesses |
| **Pain Point** | A problem or frustration your customer has that your product solves |
| **Buyer Journey** | The steps a customer takes from "I have a problem" to "I bought this product" |
| **Economic Buyer** | The person who controls the budget and ultimately approves the purchase |
| **Champion** | The person inside a company who loves your product and pushes for it internally |
| **AI Readiness** | How prepared a customer is to adopt and use AI tools in their work |
| **LLM** | Large Language Model — the AI technology behind tools like ChatGPT and Claude |
| **Churn** | When a customer stops using (and paying for) your product |
| **MRR** | Monthly Recurring Revenue — predictable monthly income from subscriptions |
| **ARR** | Annual Recurring Revenue — MRR × 12, the yearly version |
| **POC** | Proof of Concept — a small test to prove your product works before a full purchase |
| **PMF** | Product-Market Fit — the point where your product perfectly solves your ICP's need |
| **GTM** | Go-To-Market — your plan for how to launch and sell your product |
| **B2B** | Business-to-Business — you sell your product to other companies |
| **B2C** | Business-to-Consumer — you sell your product directly to individual people |
| **SOC 2** | A security compliance certification that enterprise customers often require |
| **GDPR** | EU privacy regulation that governs how you handle customer data |
| **AI-DDLC** | AI-Driven Development Life Cycle — a modern development process where AI assists at every phase |
| **Trigger Event** | Something that happens in a customer's life or company that makes them ready to buy NOW |
