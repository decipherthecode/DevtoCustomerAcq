> ## 📖 HOW TO USE THIS DOCUMENT
>
> **Who is this for?** Solo founders, indie hackers, and small teams building AI-powered products — from your very first idea all the way to your first 500 paying customers.
>
> **How do I fill it?**
> 1. Read each section heading and the `💡 Instructions` block inside it.
> 2. Replace every line that starts with `>` (like this one) with **your own real answer**.
> 3. Delete any section that does not apply to your product — that is totally fine.
> 4. Aim for **honest, short answers** over long, perfect-sounding ones. A bad guess is better than a blank.
> 5. Use `[TBD]` for anything you do not know yet — come back and fill it in later.
> 6. **Update this document every 4 weeks** as you learn more from real users.
>
> **Plain-language guide to terms used:**
> | Term | What it means in simple words |
> |------|-------------------------------|
> | TAM  | All the money in your market if EVERY possible customer bought from someone |
> | SAM  | The chunk of that market your product can actually serve |
> | SOM  | The realistic slice YOU can win in the next 1–3 years |
> | ICP  | Your dream customer — the one who pays fast and loves the product |
> | GTM  | Your plan for getting customers from Day 1 |
> | CAC  | How much you spend to get one customer |
> | LTV  | How much money one customer gives you over their lifetime |
> | Churn | Customers who cancel or stop using your product |
> | MRR  | Monthly Recurring Revenue — your subscription income each month |
> | ARR  | Annual Recurring Revenue — MRR × 12 |
