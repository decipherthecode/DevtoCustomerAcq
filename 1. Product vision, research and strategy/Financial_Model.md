## SECTION 5 — CUSTOMER ACQUISITION COST (CAC) & LIFETIME VALUE (LTV)

> 💡 These two numbers are the most important in all of SaaS finance.
> - **CAC** = How much money does it cost you to get ONE new paying customer?
> - **LTV** = How much total money does ONE customer give you before they cancel?
> - **Golden Rule:** LTV must be at least **3× your CAC**. If it is lower, you are losing money on every customer.

## SECTION 13 — FINANCIAL MODEL GLOSSARY

> 💡 This section explains every financial term used in this document in simple language. Perfect for sharing with someone new to startup finance.

| Term | Plain-English Definition | Example |
|---|---|---|
| **MRR** (Monthly Recurring Revenue) | The total money you collect from subscriptions this month | 50 Pro customers × $29 = $1,450 MRR |
| **ARR** (Annual Recurring Revenue) | MRR × 12 — what you'd earn in a year if nothing changed | $1,450 × 12 = $17,400 ARR |
| **Churn Rate** | The % of customers who cancel each month | 3 cancellations ÷ 60 customers = 5% churn |
| **CAC** (Customer Acquisition Cost) | Money spent to get one new paying customer | $300 ads ÷ 10 new customers = $30 CAC |
| **LTV** (Customer Lifetime Value) | Total money a customer pays before they cancel | $29/mo × 20 months average = $580 LTV |
| **LTV:CAC Ratio** | How many dollars you get back for every dollar you spend on acquiring customers | LTV $580 ÷ CAC $30 = 19:1 (excellent!) |
| **Burn Rate** | How much money you spend each month | $2,000 total monthly expenses = $2,000 burn |
| **Runway** | How many months until you run out of money | $6,000 in bank ÷ $2,000 burn = 3 months |
| **Break-Even** | The point where revenue = expenses (zero profit, zero loss) | 87 customers × $29 = $2,523 = total monthly costs |
| **Gross Margin** | Revenue minus direct costs (AI, hosting), as a % | ($29 revenue − $5 AI cost) ÷ $29 = 83% gross margin |
| **ARPU** (Average Revenue Per User) | Average monthly payment across all Pro customers | Mix of monthly/annual subscribers, blended |
| **Freemium** | Offer a free version to attract users, then convert them to paid | Free: 50 AI calls; Pro: unlimited |
| **Conversion Rate** | % of free users who upgrade to paid | 5 upgrades ÷ 100 free users = 5% conversion |
| **NRR** (Net Revenue Retention) | Did your existing customers pay you more or less this month? | 105% NRR = customers are spending more on average |
| **Fixed Costs** | Costs that stay the same no matter how many customers you have | Hosting $20/month — same whether you have 1 or 1,000 users |
| **Variable Costs** | Costs that grow as your customer count grows | AI API cost grows as more users make AI calls |
| **Contribution Margin** | Revenue from 1 customer minus variable costs for that customer | $29 revenue − $4 AI − $0.84 Stripe = $24.16 contribution |
