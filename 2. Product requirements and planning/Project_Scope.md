# 📋 How to Fill This Document
> **Read this first — it will save you a lot of confusion!**

Welcome! This is your **Project Scope Document** — the single source of truth for your entire project.
Think of it like a blueprint for a house: before anyone picks up a hammer, everyone agrees on exactly what is being built, why, how much it costs, and when it'll be done.

---

## 🟢 Instructions (Beginner-Friendly)

Follow these 5 simple rules while filling out this document:

**Rule 1 — Replace the placeholder text.**
Every line that starts with `>` is a hint telling you what to write. Replace it (and the `>` symbol) with your own answer.

**Rule 2 — Write "TBD" if you don't know something yet.**
"TBD" stands for "To Be Determined." It means "I'll fill this in later." That's okay! Don't leave a field blank — write TBD so you remember to come back.

**Rule 3 — Keep it simple.**
You don't need fancy words. Write like you're explaining your project to a friend. Clear and simple beats long and confusing every time.

**Rule 4 — Update the Change Log every time you edit this file.**
The Change Log is at the very top. Every time you make a big change, add one row to that table so you always know the history of this document.

**Rule 5 — Use the ✅ checkboxes to track progress.**
Every phase has checkboxes. Check them off as you complete each task.

> A "prompt" is the instruction you give the AI. Better prompts = better AI outputs = happier users.
> Fill out one worksheet for EACH major AI feature in your product.

---

#### How Prompts Work (Quick Explanation)

When you use an AI API, you send two types of messages:

1. **System Prompt** — Secret instructions the AI always follows (users don't see this)
   Example: *"You are a helpful assistant for TaskFlow. Always respond in bullet points. Never discuss competitor products."*

2. **User Message** — What the user types or what your app sends to the AI
   Example: *"Help me plan my week. I have 5 meetings and 3 deadlines."*

3. **AI Response** — What the AI says back

**Prompt Improvement Tips (Quick Reference):**

| Problem | Fix |
|---------|-----|
| AI goes off-topic | Add "Only respond to questions about [topic]. If asked about anything else, politely redirect." |
| AI response is too long | Add "Keep your response to [X] sentences maximum." |
| AI response is too vague | Add "Be specific. Include concrete examples." |
| AI makes up facts | Add "Only use information provided by the user. Do not invent statistics or facts." |
| AI forgets the format | Add "IMPORTANT: Always format your response as [format]." at the end of your system prompt |
| AI is too formal | Add "Write in a friendly, conversational tone as if you're texting a friend." |
| AI is too casual | Add "Write in a professional, business-appropriate tone." |
