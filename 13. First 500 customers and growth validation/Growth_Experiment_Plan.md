# HOW TO FILL THIS DOCUMENT (1-minute read)

**What is it?** A **Growth Experiment Plan** is your plan for winning your first **500 customers** by running small, safe tests (**experiments**) and learning from each one. It covers the whole AI-Driven Development Life Cycle: **build → test → launch → grow to 500**.

> 🧒 **In kid words:** You have a guess about what will make people love your product. You test it small, write down what happened, then decide: keep going, change it, or stop.

## The 7 rules
1. **Answer every question.** Not relevant? Write `N/A` and one short reason.
2. **Do not guess.** Do not know? Write `UNKNOWN` and add it to Section 14.1.
3. **Use numbers and dates**, not feelings. Write `20 signups by 30 Nov 2026`, not `a lot, soon`.
4. **One experiment changes one thing.**
5. **Fill in order**: Part A (Setup) → Part B (Roadmap) → Part C (Tracking).
6. **Replace every `<<like this>>`** with your answer. Text after `Example:` is a guide; replace it.
7. **A human approves** anything that spends money, contacts real people, or uses personal data, even if an AI agent prepared it.

## R1. Glossary (words explained simply)

| Word | Meaning in plain English |
|---|---|
| **AARRR** | The five steps of the customer journey: Acquisition, Activation, Retention, Referral, Revenue. |
| **A/B test** | Show version A to half the people and version B to the other half, then see which does better. |
| **Acquisition** | How people find you. |
| **Activation** | A new person has a good first experience and reaches the "Aha moment". |
| **Aha moment** | The moment a customer first sees why your product is useful. |
| **AI agent** | A computer program powered by AI that can do tasks for you, like writing drafts or checking data. |
| **AI-DLC** | AI-Driven Development Life Cycle: building and growing a product from idea to launch with AI helping at each step. |
| **ASO** | App Store Optimization: making your app easy to find in app stores. |
| **Baseline** | Your starting number before you change anything. |
| **Beta** | An early version tested by a small group of real people. |
| **CAC** | Customer Acquisition Cost: money spent to win one customer. |
| **Channel** | A place where you reach customers (social media, email, ads, and so on). |
| **Cohort** | A group of customers who joined in the same time period. |
| **Confidence** | How sure you are about your guess (used in RICE). |
| **Consent** | A person clearly saying "yes, you may use my data." |
| **Control** | The unchanged version you compare against. |
| **CRM** | Customer Relationship Management: a tool where you keep track of people you talk to and sell to. |
| **Effort** | How much work something takes (used in RICE). |
| **Experiment** | A small, safe test to learn if an idea works. |
| **Funnel** | The path customers walk from finding you to paying you. |
| **Gate** | A checkpoint at the end of a phase: Go, Fix, or Stop. |
| **Guardrail metric** | A safety number that must NOT get worse while you test (for example, the number of complaints). |
| **Hypothesis** | A clear guess that can be proven right or wrong: "We believe X will cause Y because Z." |
| **ICP** | Ideal Customer Profile: the kind of person who needs your product most. |
| **Impact** | How much an idea could help (used in RICE). |
| **LTV** | Lifetime Value: total money one customer pays you. |
| **North Star Metric** | The one number that best shows customers get value. |
| **PMF** | Product-Market Fit: enough people truly want your product. |
| **RACI** | A chart that says who is Responsible, Accountable, Consulted, and Informed for each job. |
| **Reach** | How many people an idea touches (used in RICE). |
| **Referral** | A customer telling others about you. |
| **Retention** | Customers coming back and staying. |
| **RICE** | A score to rank ideas: (Reach × Impact × Confidence) ÷ Effort. |
| **Rollback** | Undoing a change quickly if something breaks. |
| **Sample size** | How many people you need in a test to trust the result. |
| **SEO** | Search Engine Optimization: making your website easy to find on Google and other search engines. |
| **Stop-loss** | A rule that says when to end a test early to avoid damage. |
| **UNKNOWN** | The word you write when you do not know an answer. Never guess. List it in Section 14.1. |
| **Waitlist** | A list of people who want your product before it is ready. |

# 11. RANK YOUR IDEAS WITH THE RICE SCORE

> 🧒 You will have many ideas. RICE gives each idea a score so you can test the best ones first.

## 11.1 How to score (use these scales exactly)
| Letter | Means | How to fill | Scale |
|---|---|---|---|
| **R** Reach | How many people will this touch in one phase? | Write a real number | e.g. 200 |
| **I** Impact | How much will it move the goal for each person? | Pick one | 3 = massive, 2 = high, 1 = medium, 0.5 = low, 0.25 = tiny |
| **C** Confidence | How sure are you about R and I? | Pick one | 100% = we have data, 80% = some evidence, 50% = a guess, below 50% = a wild guess |
