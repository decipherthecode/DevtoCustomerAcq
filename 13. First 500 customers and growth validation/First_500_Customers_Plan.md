# How to Fill This Document

**Read this box first. It takes 2 minutes.**

## What this document is
A step-by-step plan that takes your product from **idea → built → launched → your first 500 customers**. It is a questionnaire. You answer the questions; the plan writes itself.

## The 5 rules
1. **Answer every question.** Write your answer after `➡️ ANSWER:`. Never leave it empty.
2. **Don't know yet?** Write `UNKNOWN – [who will find out] – [by what date]`. That is a valid answer. A blank is not.
3. **Not needed for your product?** Write `N/A – [one-line reason]`.
4. **Use plain words and real numbers.** Not "many users", but "50 users". Not "soon", but "2026-11-30".
5. **Copy the examples, then change them.** Every question has an `Example:` line. Replace it with your own truth.

## What the symbols mean
| Symbol | Meaning |
|---|---|
| ➡️ ANSWER: | Type your answer here |
| ☐ | A checkbox. Change to ☑ when done |
| 🤖 AI AGENT BLOCK | Copy the prompt into your AI agent. Paste its output back here |
| ✋ HUMAN CHECKPOINT | A real person must say "approved" before moving on |
| 🚦 GATE | A stop/go decision. Do not pass until every box is ☑ |

## How to work through it
- **Fast path (about 30 minutes):** Fill only the items marked **⭐ Quick Start**. You get a rough but usable plan.
- **Full path:** Fill everything, one section at a time, in order. Don't skip ahead.
- **Update it weekly.** Change the `Status` line at the top and add one line to the Decision Log (Section 11).

## Words you might not know
| Word | Plain meaning |
|---|---|
| **Customer** | Someone who pays (or, if the product is free, someone who uses it for its main purpose at least twice). You decide in Section 1. |
| **Activation** | The first moment a person gets real value from your product |
| **Retention** | People coming back after their first visit |
| **Churn** | People who leave and stop using it |
| **MVP** | The smallest version that solves the main problem |
| **Beta** | A test version given to a small group before everyone |
| **AI agent** | An AI tool (such as Claude) that does tasks for you, step by step |
| **Gate** | A checkpoint where you decide: Go, Hold, or Stop |

## Who does what (fill once, reuse everywhere)
- **Owner:** the one person who is responsible for the whole plan.
- **Approver:** the person who says "approved" at ✋ checkpoints. Can be the Owner.
- **AI agents:** do drafting, building, testing, and number-crunching. They **never** approve their own work.

## Ground rules for AI agents (paste at the start of every agent chat)
> You are helping me with [Product name from Section 1]. Use only facts from this document. If a fact is missing, ask me, do not guess. Show your work and list any assumptions. Never publish, send messages, spend money, or delete anything without my written "approved". Keep language simple.

## 2. Customer & Problem (Validation) ⭐ Quick Start

**Why this matters:** Most products fail because nobody needed them, not because the code was bad. Prove the problem is real **before** you build much.

**Rule:** Friends being polite is not proof. Proof is a stranger who feels the pain, or who pays.

1. **Who has the problem? Be specific.** (Not "everyone".)
   ➡️ ANSWER: *Example: "Busy city workers aged 25–40 who own 5+ houseplants and keep killing them."*
2. **What is the problem, in their words?**
   ➡️ ANSWER: *Example: "I forget to water my plants and they die."*
3. **How do they solve it today?** (This is your real competition, even if it is a notebook.)
   ➡️ ANSWER: *Example: Phone reminders, sticky notes, or they just give up.*
4. **How painful is it?** (1 = annoying, 5 = they would pay today)
   ➡️ ANSWER: *Example: 3, many care but few have paid for a fix.*
5. **How many people did you talk to, and who?** (Aim for at least 10 who are NOT friends or family.)

| # | Who (role, not private details) | Date | Their biggest pain | Would they pay? (Y/N) |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | ➡️ | ➡️ | ➡️ | ➡️ |
| 2 | ➡️ | ➡️ | ➡️ | ➡️ |
| 3 | ➡️ | ➡️ | ➡️ | ➡️ |

6. **Strongest proof so far?** ☐ Pre-orders ☐ Paid pilots ☐ Signed letters of intent ☐ Waitlist with names ☐ Only conversations ☐ None yet
   ➡️ ANSWER: *Example: 3 strangers prepaid $10 for early access.*
7. **Where do these people hang out?** (sites, groups, places, events)
   ➡️ ANSWER: *Example: r/houseplants, Instagram plant creators, local plant shops.*
8. **What would make you WRONG?** (Write the result that would make you stop or change direction.)
   ➡️ ANSWER: *Example: If fewer than 3 of 20 people say they'd pay, rethink the idea.*

> **Magic question to ask every early customer:** "Who else has this problem?" Write the names you get here: ➡️

🤖 **AI AGENT BLOCK: Validation helper**
- **Give the agent:** answers to questions 1–8.
- **Prompt to copy:** *"Read my answers in Section 2. List my 5 riskiest guesses. For each, suggest the cheapest test (under 1 week, under $50). Write 10 non-leading interview questions about the problem, not my solution. Flag anything that sounds like polite feedback instead of proof."*
- **Agent output goes here:** ➡️
- **Must not do:** invent quotes, interviews, or survey results.

🚦 **GATE 1: Problem is real.** Pass only if all are ☑
- ☐ At least 10 non-friend conversations logged
- ☐ At least 3 people said they would pay, or already paid
- ☐ The "what would make me wrong" test did not trigger
- ✋ **Approver says:** ☐ GO ☐ HOLD (fix and retry) ☐ STOP

## 3. Offer, Price & Promise ⭐ Quick Start

**Why this matters:** People buy a result, not a feature list.

1. **What result does the customer get?** (one sentence, starting with a verb)
   ➡️ ANSWER: *Example: "Keep every plant alive with a 10-second daily check."*
2. **Why you and not the alternatives?** (one reason)
   ➡️ ANSWER: *Example: "It learns each plant's needs from a photo."*
3. **What does it cost, and how do people pay?** ☐ Free ☐ One-time ☐ Monthly ☐ Yearly ☐ Usage-based ☐ Other
   ➡️ ANSWER: *Example: $5 per month, first 14 days free.*
4. **Will early customers pay?** (Recommended: yes, even a small price, because it proves real interest.)
   ➡️ ANSWER: *Example: Yes. First 100 get $3 per month locked for life.*
5. **What special thing do your first 100 get that later customers won't?**
   ➡️ ANSWER: *Example: Founder's badge, direct chat with Priya, early voting on features.*
6. **Refund / cancel rule, in one line.**
   ➡️ ANSWER: *Example: Cancel any time. Full refund within 14 days.*
7. **Landing page headline and call-to-action button text.**
   ➡️ ANSWER: *Example: "Never kill a plant again." Button: "Start free".*
8. **If you have a free plan, what is free and what is paid?** (Free should show the value; paid should unlock more of it.)
   ➡️ ANSWER: *Example: Free = up to 3 plants. Paid = unlimited plants, photo plant-ID.* (Write N/A if only paid.)

## 4. Build Plan (AI-Driven Development)

**Why this matters:** AI agents do most of the building work. Humans decide, check, and approve. Specifications guide the agents. Humans review the plan, not only the finished result.

**The golden rules**
- Agents propose. Humans approve at every ✋.
- Anything touching **payments, logins, personal data, or security** always needs human review, however confident the agent sounds.
- "Done" means there is **evidence** (tests passed, screenshots, logs), not just the agent saying so.

### 4.1 The MVP (smallest useful version) ⭐ Quick Start
1. **List the 3–5 must-have features.** (If a customer can't get the promise from Section 3 without it, it is a must-have.)
   ➡️ ANSWER: *Example: 1) Add a plant 2) Daily reminder 3) Mark as watered 4) Sign-up and pay.*
2. **List what you will NOT build yet.**
   ➡️ ANSWER: *Example: Social sharing, shop, dark mode.*
3. **What is the one action that shows a user got value?** (This is "activation", used again in Section 8.)
   ➡️ ANSWER: *Example: "Added a plant and marked it watered once."*
4. **Tools and tech chosen?**
   ➡️ ANSWER: *Example: Next.js, Supabase, Stripe, hosted on Vercel.*
5. **Does the product use AI inside it?** ☐ No ☐ Yes → what are the risks (wrong answers, cost per use, private data)?
   ➡️ ANSWER: *Example: Yes. Photo plant-ID may be wrong; cost about $0.02 per photo; photos are private.*

### 4.2 Build phases
Fill the table, then work top to bottom. Each row has a "Done when" rule so nobody has to guess.

| Phase | AI agent does | Human does | Done when | Status |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| A. Spec | Turns Section 1–4.1 into user stories and acceptance rules | Reads and corrects them | Every must-have has a testable rule | ☐ |
| B. Design | Proposes screens, data, and structure | Picks and approves | One clear design approved | ☐ |
| C. Build | Writes code in small steps | Reviews each change | All must-haves work end to end | ☐ |

## 7. Getting Customers 1 → 500 ⭐ Quick Start

|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | 1–10 | Personal outreach, warm introductions, interview list | ➡️ *Example: direct messages* | ➡️ |
| 2 | 11–50 | Ask early customers "who else has this problem?"; communities; content | ➡️ | ➡️ |
| 3 | 51–100 | Pick the 1–2 channels that worked; repeat them | ➡️ | ➡️ |
| 4 | 101–500 | Referrals, partners, content, small paid tests | ➡️ | ➡️ |

### 10.2a Unit economics: does each customer earn more than they cost? ⭐

| **B.** Cost to serve one customer per month | Hosting + AI usage + support ÷ customers | ➡️ *Example: $1.20* | ➡️ |
| **C.** Profit per customer per month | A − B | ➡️ | ➡️ |
| **D.** Cost to win one customer | Marketing + sales spend ÷ new customers | ➡️ *Example: $20* | ➡️ |
| **E.** Months they stay on average | 1 ÷ monthly churn rate (for example 10% churn → 10 months) | ➡️ *Example: 10* | ➡️ |
| **F.** Total profit from one customer | C × E | ➡️ | ➡️ |
| **G.** Payback time | D ÷ C (months to earn back D) | ➡️ | ➡️ |
| **H.** Is F bigger than D? (A common rule of thumb is about 3 times bigger. Use your own target.) | F ÷ D | ➡️ | ➡️ |

## Appendix: Quick reference

**The whole journey in one line:** Prove the problem → define the offer → build with AI under human approval → test with beta users → launch → win customers 1–10 by hand → find a repeatable channel for 10–100 → grow to 500 → review numbers weekly.

**The 5 questions to ask when stuck**
1. Who exactly is this for?
2. What proof do I have that they'd pay?
3. What is the next smallest step?
4. What number will tell me if it worked?
5. Who needs to approve before I go on?

**Reusing this template on a new project:** Copy this file, rename it for the new product, clear every ➡️ ANSWER and ☑, keep the example lines as hints, reset the Gate Tracker, and start at Section 1.
