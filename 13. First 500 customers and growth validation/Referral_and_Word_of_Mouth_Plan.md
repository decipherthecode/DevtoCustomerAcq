# HOW TO FILL THIS DOCUMENT (READ THIS FIRST)

**What is this?** A plan for getting your first 500 customers to tell other people about your product. "Word of mouth" means people talk about you on their own. A "referral" means you give people a reason and a way to invite friends.

**Who can fill it?** Anyone. If you can answer "who, what, why, how much, by when," you can finish this. If a word is new, check the **Glossary (Appendix B)**.

### The 7 rules

1. **Go in order.** Start at Section 0 and finish at Section 17. Later sections use your earlier answers.
2. **Answer every question.** Type on the line that says `✍️ Your answer:`. Delete the grey example text only if it doesn't apply to you.
3. **Never guess.** If you don't know, write `UNKNOWN - find out by [date] - [who]`. A written unknown is fine. A hidden guess is not.
4. **Use numbers, not feelings.** Write "20 people," not "a lot." Write "by 30 Nov," not "soon."
5. **Look at the tags.**
   - 🟢 **Required**: the plan is not valid without it.
   - 🟡 **Recommended**: skip only with a written reason.
   - ⚪ **Optional**: fill in if it applies.
   - 🤖 **AI-optional**: for teams that use AI agents. Humans-only teams can skip these. Skipping them never makes the plan invalid.
6. **Tick the boxes.** Change `[ ]` to `[x]` when done.
7. **Finish with Section 17.** The final checklist tells you whether the plan is ready to use.

### Copying this template for a new project

1. Copy this file. Rename it `Referral_and_Word_of_Mouth_Plan_[ProjectName].md`.
2. Fill in the **Document Control** block below.
3. In **Section 5H**, tick which program variants apply (standard, B2B/partner, offline/local, marketplace). Fill only the variant sections you ticked.
4. Work top to bottom. Do not delete sections. Mark them "Not applicable - [reason]" instead, so the next reader knows it was a decision and not an accident.

> **Not legal or financial advice.** Section 12 is a checklist to help you ask the right questions. Have a qualified person review anything involving money, personal data, or advertising rules in your country.

# SECTION 8: MATH HELPERS 🔢 🟢

*Fill in the blanks. A worked example is below each formula so you can copy the steps.*

### 8.1 How much can we afford to pay per referred customer?

**Formula:**

```
Max reward (per side) = (LTV x target profit share) - other cost to win that customer
```

| Part | Meaning | ✍️ Your number |
|---|---|---|
| LTV | Money one customer pays us over their life | |
| Target profit share | The share of that money we want to keep as profit (e.g. 70% = 0.70) | |
| Other cost | Other cost to win them (ads, tools, staff time) | |
| **Max reward** | The answer | |

💡 *Example:* LTV is $120. We want to keep 70%, so $120 x 0.70 = $84. Other costs are $14. So max reward = $84 - $14 = **$70 total to split.** If it's two-sided, we might give $15 to each side, well under the limit.

### 8.2 How fast does the program spread? (K-factor)

**Formula:**

```
K = (invites sent per customer) x (share of invites that become customers)
```

| Part | ✍️ Your number |
|---|---|
| Average invites sent per customer | |
| Share of invites that turn into customers (as a decimal, 10% = 0.10) | |
| **K** | |

💡 *Example:* Each customer sends 4 invites. 10% of invites join. K = 4 x 0.10 = **0.4.**

**How to read it:**
- K above 1: every customer brings more than one new customer. It grows by itself. This is very rare.
- K below 1: referrals help, but you still need other ways to grow. That is normal and still valuable.

### 8.3 How many days does one loop take? (cycle time)

```
Cycle time = average days from "customer joins" to "their friend joins"
```

✍️ Your number: ______ days. Shorter is better.

### 8.4 Net Promoter Score (NPS)

Ask: **"How likely are you to recommend us to a friend or colleague? 0 to 10."**

```
Promoters = people who answered 9 or 10
Detractors = people who answered 0 to 6
(7 and 8 are "passive" and are not counted)

NPS = % Promoters - % Detractors
```

| Part | ✍️ Your number |
|---|---|
| People who answered | |
| Promoters (9 to 10) | |
| Detractors (0 to 6) | |
| NPS | |

💡 *Example:* 50 people answer. 28 give 9 or 10 (56%). 8 give 0 to 6 (16%). NPS = 56 - 16 = **40.**

⚠️ With few answers, the score jumps around. Early on, read the written comments more than the number.

### 8.5 Cost to win a referred customer (referral CAC) and payback

```
Referral CAC = (total rewards paid + tool cost + staff cost for referrals) / number of referred customers
Payback months = Referral CAC / monthly profit per customer
```

| Part | ✍️ Your number |
|---|---|
| Rewards paid this period | |
| Tool and staff cost | |
| Referred customers this period | |
| **Referral CAC** | |
| Monthly profit per customer | |
| **Payback months** | |

💡 *Example:* We paid $300 in rewards, $100 in tools and time, and won 20 referred customers. CAC = $400 / 20 = **$20.** Each customer gives $10 profit a month. Payback = **2 months.**

### 8.6 Share of growth from referrals and word of mouth

```
Referral share = referred customers / all new customers x 100
```

*Rough guide only (not a promise):* about 10% of new customers from referrals is often seen as a healthy early sign. Your market may differ.

```

```

### 8.8 Fraud loss rate
