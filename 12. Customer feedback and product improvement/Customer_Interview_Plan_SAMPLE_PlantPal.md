# PART 2. PRODUCT SNAPSHOT

**2.1** Our product helps **busy plant owners** to **remember when to water each plant** so that **their plants stay alive**.

**2.2 Problem today:** People forget to water houseplants, or water too much because they follow a fixed schedule. They guess, and plants die. In Phase 1, 8 of 12 people described losing at least one plant in the last 6 months.

**2.3 What people use today:** ☑ Another app ☑ Spreadsheet or notes ☑ Asking a person ☑ Doing nothing ☐ Other
Names of the alternatives: a generic reminders app, sticky notes on pots, asking a plant-shop employee, "just checking when I walk past."

**2.4 Stage:** ☑ Just an idea ☐ Prototype ☐ Working beta ☐ Launched ☐ Growing

**2.5 Biggest unknown:** We are unsure whether people care enough to open an app every week, or whether a reminder alone is enough.

# PART 3. GOALS AND LEARNING QUESTIONS

**3.1 Big goal:** By **30 June 2027**, we want **500 customers using PlantPal at least once a week** so that we know people really need it.

**3.2 Decisions these interviews will help us make:**
☑ Should we build this at all? ☑ Which feature comes first? ☐ What should it cost? ☑ Who is our best first customer? ☑ Is it easy enough to use? ☐ What stops people signing up? ☑ Why do people stop using it? ☑ What should we fix next?

**3.3 Learning questions**

| # | Learning question | Why it matters | Phase |
|---|---|---|---|
| L1 | How do people decide when to water a plant today? | Shows whether reminders solve a real problem | 1 |
| L2 | What happens when a plant dies, and what do people do next? | Shows how painful the problem is | 1 |
| L3 | Can new users add a plant and get a first reminder without help? | Tests the core flow | 2 |
| L4 | What makes someone keep using it after week 1? | Drives retention | 3 to 5 |
| L5 | What do people already pay for plant care? | Informs price | 3 |

**3.4 Guesses**

| # | We believe that... | Right if... | Wrong if... |
|---|---|---|---|
| H1 | People who lost a plant in the last 6 months want help | 6 of 10 describe a specific recent loss | fewer than 3 of 10 do |
| H2 | Overwatering matters as much as forgetting | 4 of 10 mention overwatering unprompted | fewer than 2 of 10 do |
| H3 | New plant owners are our best first customers | They describe more losses than experienced owners | Experienced owners describe more |

**3.5 Not trying to learn yet:** Prices of competing apps, and any marketing slogans. Those are for the Beta phase.

# PART 6. PHASE PLANS

## PHASE 1: DISCOVER (completed)

- **Goal:** Learn whether real people have this problem, how bad it is, and how they cope today.
- **Who and how many:** S1 (7 people), S2 (3 people), S3 (2 people). **12 interviews.**
- **Methods:** ☑ Video ☑ Phone ☐ In-person ☑ Async/written
- **Question bank used:** Story questions ("Tell me about the last time you lost a plant"), pain and workarounds, closing referral question (see template 6.1.4).
- **Commitment signals:** 5 agreed to test a prototype; 3 introduced a friend; 0 paid (too early).

**🚦 GATE 1: Ready to design and build?**
- ☑ We did at least **12** interviews (target 10+)
- ☑ **8** people described a specific recent story (target 6+)
- ☑ Top 3 pains in their own words: "I forget until the leaves droop"; "I water on Sundays no matter what"; "I don't know what each plant needs"
- ☑ We know what people use today (generic reminders, sticky notes, asking friends)
- ☑ First customer group: S1 New plant owners
- ☑ **5** people took a real action (agreed to test a prototype)
- ☑ Insights logged in Part 13

**Decision:** ☑ Go ☐ Change direction ☐ Stop. Reason: H1 supported (8 of 12); H2 supported (6 of 12 mentioned overwatering unprompted); strong referral signal.
