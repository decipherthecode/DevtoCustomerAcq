# HOW TO FILL THIS DOCUMENT (READ THIS FIRST)

> **Folder:** `Launch, marketing and customer acquisition/FAQ_and_Knowledge_Base.md`
> **Template version:** 1.0
> **Covers:** AI-Driven Development Life Cycle, from building the product to the day your **first 500 customers** are using it.
> **Works for:** web apps, SaaS, mobile apps, or any product.

## What is this document?

This is one file that helps you build two things for your product:

1. **An FAQ**: short questions and answers people see quickly ("How much does it cost?").
2. **A Knowledge Base (KB)**: longer help articles that explain how to do things ("How to connect your calendar").

You do not need to be technical. If you can answer a question in plain words, you can fill this in.

## The 7 simple rules

1. **Go in order.** Start at Section 0 and move down. Do not skip around the first time.
2. **Answer every question.** Write your answer after the `ANSWER:` label. Replace the underscores `____`.
3. **Use the examples.** Every question has an `EXAMPLE:`. Copy the *style*, not the words.
4. **Tick the boxes.** Change `[ ]` to `[x]` to choose. Pick only what is true today.
5. **Do not guess.** If you do not know, write `UNKNOWN` and add it to the **Open Questions list** (Section 22). Never invent facts, prices, or promises.
6. **Finish each "DONE WHEN" box.** It tells you when a section is truly complete.
7. **Use short sentences.** If a 10-year-old cannot read it, rewrite it.

## Symbols used in this document

| Symbol | Meaning |
|---|---|
| `ANSWER:` | You write here |
| `EXAMPLE:` | A sample answer to copy the style from |
| `[ ]` / `[x]` | Not done / done (or not chosen / chosen) |
| `CHOOSE ONE:` | Tick exactly one box |
| `CHOOSE ALL THAT APPLY:` | Tick as many as are true |
| `AI PROMPT:` | Copy this into your AI tool (Claude or similar) to get a first draft. **A human must always check the result.** |
| `DONE WHEN:` | The finish line for the section |
| `UNKNOWN` | You do not know yet. Put it in Section 22 |

## Time needed

| How you fill it | Time |
|---|---|
| Quick pass (Sections 0 to 3 only) | 30 minutes |
| Full first pass (everything) | 3 to 5 hours, spread over days |
| Keep it updated during growth | 30 minutes per week |

## When to fill which part (match it to your project stage)

| Your project stage | Fill these sections |
|---|---|
| Building the product | 0, 1, 2, 3, 4, 5 |
| Getting ready to launch (beta, waitlist) | 6, 7, 8, 9, 10, 11, 12, 13 |
| Launch week | 14, 15, 16, 17 |
| Growing to 500 customers | 18, 19, 20, 21 |

## Golden safety rules for AI help

- Never paste passwords, API keys, or customers' private data into an AI tool.
- An AI answer is a **draft**, not a fact. Check prices, dates, legal text, and steps yourself.
- If the product does not do something, the FAQ must say so honestly.

ID: KB-FIX-001
Category: Troubleshooting
Audience: New user
Applies to: iPhone, Android | Plan needed: Free or Plus
Last checked: 2026-03-12 by: Maya
Status: Live

## Quick answer
Reminders usually stop because phone notifications are turned off. Turning them on takes about 30 seconds.
