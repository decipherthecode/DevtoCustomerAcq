# HOW TO FILL THIS DOCUMENT (READ THIS FIRST)

This file is a **questionnaire**. You answer the questions, and at the end you have a complete plan for launching your product and getting your **first 500 customers**. A 10-year-old can fill it in if they read the rules below.

## The 7 Golden Rules

1. **Go top to bottom.** Later sections use your earlier answers.
2. **Replace every `[ANSWER]` with your own words.** Short is fine. One sentence is often enough.
3. **Never leave a blank.** If you do not know, write `UNKNOWN - find out by [date]`. If it does not apply, write `N/A - [one-line reason]`.
4. **Use numbers, not feelings.** Write "100 people", not "a lot". Write "by 15 March", not "soon".
5. **Look at the EXAMPLE under each question.** Copy its style, not its words.
6. **Tick the boxes.** Change `[ ]` to `[x]` when something is done or chosen.
7. **If a question confuses you,** use the AI helper prompts in Section 21 or ask a friend. A good answer is one a stranger could read and understand.

## Fill-in markers (what the symbols mean)

| Symbol | Meaning |
|---|---|
| `[ANSWER]` | Type your answer here |
| `[ ]` / `[x]` | Not done / Done (or not chosen / chosen) |
| 🔴 **REQUIRED** | Must be filled before launch |
| 🟡 **RECOMMENDED** | Skip only with a written reason |
| 🟢 **OPTIONAL** | Fill if it helps you |
| ⛔ **GATE** | Stop sign. Do not go to the next phase until every box is ticked |

## Who fills what (in 4 steps)

1. **Fill Sections 0-6** (about 60-90 minutes). This is the "thinking" part.
2. **Fill Sections 7-13** (about 2-3 hours). This is the "planning" part.
3. **Fill Sections 14-20** (about 1-2 hours). This is the "safety and running" part.
4. **Give the whole file to your AI agent** with the handoff block in Section 21. Review what it builds.

## How to keep it healthy

- **One source of truth.** This is the only launch plan. Do not make a second copy.
- **Update the Change Log (Section 24)** every time you edit something important.
- **Review weekly** during launch. Update the Customer Counter (Section 9).
- **Version names:** `v1.0` first complete plan, `v1.1` small fixes, `v2.0` big change of direction.

## What "done" looks like

The document is ready when:
- [ ] No `[ANSWER]` is left anywhere.
- [ ] A stranger can read Sections 1-3 and explain your product in 30 seconds.
- [ ] Every goal has a number and a date.
- [ ] Every task has one named owner (a person or an AI agent).
- [ ] The Final Checklist (Section 23) is all ticked.

# 22. GLOSSARY (PLAIN WORDS)

| Word | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **AI-DLC** | AI-Driven Development Life Cycle: building a product from idea to launch with AI helping at each stage |
| **Aha moment** | The first moment a user truly sees the value |
| **Beta** | An early test version used by a small group before everyone |
| **Call to action (CTA)** | The button or sentence that asks people to do something |
| **Channel** | A place where we reach customers (a community, email, search, social, ads) |
| **Conversion** | A visitor doing the thing you want (sign up, pay) |
| **Customer** | Someone who meets our definition in Section 2.1 |
| **Funnel** | The steps from first visit to becoming a happy customer |
| **Gate** | A checklist you must finish before moving to the next stage |
| **ICP (ideal customer profile)** | The best-fit customer you describe in Section 3 |
| **Landing page** | A web page made to convince someone to take one action |
| **Launch** | The day the product opens to the public |
| **MVP** | The smallest version that is useful and sellable |
| **Onboarding** | Helping new users get started |
| **Product-market fit** | When enough people really need your product that they'd be upset to lose it |
| **Retention** | People coming back and staying |
| **Rollback** | Undoing a release to go back to the last working version |
| **ROI** | Return on investment: what you get back vs. what you spent |
| **UTM tag** | A small tag added to a link so you know which channel sent a visitor |
| **Waitlist** | A list of people who asked to be told when you launch |
